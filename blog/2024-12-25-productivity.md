Since the pandemic hit the world, I have noticed that from time to time I end up feeling overwhelmed by work and I haven't yet found the perfect solution for this. I think that on our actual society we have a problem related to what means to be productive, many times, being productive means that we don't stop doing things from our entire week. This doesn't make sense because having time off can make us bored and help us think and maybe arrive at conclusions that are better for what we are doing right now.

The interesting part is that productivity is somewhat tied to what the individual observe into his own world, it leans over toward the goals of oneself. But can we thinkerers be 100% productive on the broather term? and what is this broather term?

In `Slow Productivity` there is one chapter talking about that for thinking workers since the beginning of the industrial age, it has been difficult to measure productivity, and given that we are using other alternatives to reflect that. Like the presence in the office. And this would lead to the idea that if they are there, they must be doing something.
